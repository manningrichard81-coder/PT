Today. It was a busy day in the office, but I still got out. I parked near where I used to live and ran an old route: down to the beach, along the coastal path, then up the big stairs. Two weeks ago I had to stop before the top. This time I made it all the way up, kept running, and carried on back to the car. It was one of my favourite runs in a long time.

The takeaway. Not many people could walk out the door and do that. There’s fitness there, a real base, and I get to build on it.

Watch out. For the last few days I’ve been reaching for YouTube Shorts more and more, chasing cheap dopamine. I’ve noticed it, so I’m writing it down.

Home. We had dinner in the garden with the kids. I felt present for it.
