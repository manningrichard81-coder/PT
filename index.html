The Program

4-Week Iron Protocol

Four structured weeks built to rewire your habits and rebuild your body. Expect to get uncomfortable. Everything you need — nothing you don't.

$400 Complete 4-week package
Week 01

Assessment & Reset

Body composition, movement screening, and baseline strength testing. We lock in your meal times, your training template, and the daily targets you'll tick off from day one.

Week 02

Move & Fuel

Drill the fundamentals — deadlift, squat, press, row. Strip back the processed rubbish and settle into the meal plan. Form before load, discipline before intensity.

Week 03

Load & Discomfort

Now it gets hard. Progressive overload, beach runs, battle ropes, ocean swims. This is the week you learn what you're actually made of. No excuses, no wagon to fall off.

Week 04

Integration & Independence

Lock in your weekly template. Retest your benchmarks. Walk away with a system you own — training, nutrition, recovery, and accountability you can run on your own.
